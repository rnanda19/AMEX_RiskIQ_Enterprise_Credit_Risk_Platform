Reported honestly, including the miss

Of the 14 problems, 9 are production-recommended value-creation systems summing to $429,926,252.73/cycle, 2 are foundational models with no standalone dollar figure, 1 (Problem 3) is a reserve-accuracy gain kept in its own category, and 1 (Problem 10) is honestly excluded — its real trend-coherence and minimum-tier-population KPI checks did not clear their own bar on the real, measured backtest, even after a documented root-cause investigation and redesign. It ships anyway, in full, for transparency — see its README and CHANGELOG for the full before/after.
